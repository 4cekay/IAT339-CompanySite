Interactive Elements

In-text link

CSS Selectors:

.list-link
.in-text-link

Sample Code:

          <ul>
  <li>
    <a>
      class="list-link"
      href="#member-a-profile"
    </a>: leader, drummer, vocalist, and composer, producer, and lyricist
  </li>

  <li>
    <a>
      class="list-link"
      href="#member-b-profile"
    </a>: lead guitarist, bassist, vocalist, and lyricist
  </li>

  <li>
    <a>
      class="list-link"
      href="#member-c-profile"
    </a>: keyboardist, rhythm guitarist, and lyricist
  </li>
</ul>
          
        
          <p>
  The music video for 
  <a
    class="in-text-link"
    href="https://www.youtube.com/"
    target="_blank"
    >[LEAD SINGLE]</a
  >
  is finally here! This has been our favourite project to date, and we hope you love it as much as we loved making it!
</p>

Rendered Elements:

Within a list of artist descriptions, leading to individual profile sections:

[ROCK BAND NAME] is a gothic rock musical group consisting of 3 members:

  • Member A: leader, drummer, vocalist, and composer, producer, and lyricist
  • Member B: lead guitarist, bassist, vocalist, and lyricist
  • Member C: keyboardist, rhythm guitarist, and lyricist
Within text on a homepage, leading to an external site (e.g. YouTube)

The music video for [LEAD SINGLE] is finally here! This has been our favourite project to date, and we hope you love it as much as we loved making it!

Navigation Link

CSS Selector:

.button-nav

Sample Code:

<a href="#latest" class="button-nav">Latest</a>

Rendered Element:

Example of a navigation bar button that would lead to a section with updates and announcements.

Text Input Field with Label

CSS Selectors:

.input-label
.input-field

Sample Code:

<label class="input-label" for="text-input">Email Address: <label>
<input id="text-input" class="input-field" type="text" placeholder="e.g. you@email.com">

Rendered Element:

Example of a text input field with a label on a newsletter subscription form:

Enter your email address below to subscribe to our newsletter and receive updates on new releases, tour dates, and exclusive content!

Submit/Reset Buttons

CSS Selectors:

.button-submit
.button-reset

Sample Code:

<input class="button-submit" type="submit" value="Submit">
<input class="button-reset" type="reset" value="Reset All Fields">

Rendered Element:

Example of submit and reset buttons on fanclub/tour presale signup forms.
