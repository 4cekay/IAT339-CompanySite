Branding

Brand Basics

Products/Services

  • Physical album copies
  • Digital music (streaming platforms, music videos)
  • Merchandise (clothing, accessories, trinkets, etc.)
  • Live performances (tour dates and tickets)
  • Fan events (meet and greets/fansigns, backstage access, livestreams)
  • Social media presence and interaction

Relationship to Customers

As artists and musicians:

  • Entertainment
  • Musical influence/inspiration
  • Source of expression, or escape from reality

As public figures:

  • Platform gives some power to open conversations about deeper topics or experiences
  • Voice that canspread awareness on important things

Consumer Rationale

Connection to the music as an art form:

  • Owning the music and artwork
  • Having collections of memorabilia art to display
  • Wanting to have discoveries and records of an artist's history

Fandom culture, community and identity:

  • Being part of a community of like-minded fans and metting with others for fan events and concerts
  • Exclusive content and experience (fanmeets, signed items, limited edition access)
  • Expressing identity and personal style through merchandise

Supporting the artist:

  • Seeing purchases as a way to directly support an artist's livelihood
  • Supporting the artist's creative vision and future projects
  • Feeling a sense of pride and accomplishment in contributing to an artist's success
  • Wanting to boost artist statistics for sales and records
